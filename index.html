Before you get in touch

A few useful answers.

Prefer to speak to us? Call 07367 079485 or use WhatsApp.

Do you buy every property yourself?

No. We may buy directly, or discuss introducing your property to suitable investors if we are not going to buy it. We explain the route and ask before sharing your contact details.

Will I receive full market value?

An off-market offer may be below open-market value. Condition, repair costs and the buyer’s investment requirements affect the price. If the highest possible sale price is your priority, compare estate agent and auction options.

Can I sell without fixing the property first?

You can discuss a property in its current condition. We assess suitability before making any commitment, so you don’t need to arrange repairs just to enquire.

How quickly could a sale complete?

Timing depends on the property, buyer, title checks and solicitor process. Tell us your preferred timetable and we’ll discuss what may be practical. We do not guarantee a completion date at enquiry stage.

Does an enquiry commit me to anything?

No. Submitting this form asks us to contact you about your property. You are not accepting an offer or agreeing to sell. Take independent advice before signing any sale agreement.

Do I need a Home Report?

Requirements depend on how the property is sold and any applicable exemption. Paying cash does not itself remove the rules. Ask your Scottish solicitor about your proposed route. Read our guide.
