Before you decide

What would make a sale work for you in Pollokshields?

You may be weighing up repair costs, an empty property, tenants or a previous sale that fell through. Describe the property and your priorities rather than committing to a selling route before you understand the terms.

Do you buy every property?

No. Suitability is assessed individually. We may buy directly or discuss a suitable investor introduction if we are not going to buy. Neither route guarantees an offer.

Is the enquiry free of obligation?

Yes. An enquiry asks us to contact you about the property. You can compare routes and decline any offer. Ask about all costs and terms before signing an agreement.

Can I enquire if I don’t live near the property?

Yes. Tell us the property’s location and your relationship to it. The buyer and your solicitor will need to confirm your authority to sell before a transaction proceeds.

How is an offer assessed?

Condition, location, likely repair costs and investment requirements affect an offer. We may request further information or a viewing. An enquiry is not an instant valuation.

When would my details be shared with investors?

We ask for permission before sharing your contact details for an investor introduction. We explain the proposed route so you can decide.
